Pro-Guide Expedition Logistics & Packing Tips

Private Custom 4x4 Land Cruiser Comfort

Our extended heavy-duty Toyota Land Cruisers feature full 360° pop-up observation hatches, guaranteed individual window seating, onboard inverter charging ports (220V/USB) for cameras and phones, and a refrigerated cooler stocked with chilled water and refreshments throughout every game drive.

Internal Bush Aviation Luggage Limitations

Light aircraft bush charters connecting remote savannah strips (e.g., Cessna Grand Caravan) enforce a strict luggage limitation of 15 kg (33 lbs) per passenger packed in soft-sided duffel bags without rigid frames or hard wheels. Complimentary secure luggage storage is provided at our Arusha and Nairobi bases for city attire.

Wildlife Photography Optics & Equipment

For savannah predator tracking and birdlife, we recommend a telephoto zoom (100–400mm or 70–200mm) paired with a wide lens (24–70mm) for landscapes. Vehicle photography beanbags are provided in every cruiser for steady telephoto support.

Savannah Climate & Layering Advice

Dawn game drives and caldera rim mornings can be crisp (12°C–14°C / 54°F–57°F), warming rapidly to 28°C (82°F) under the equatorial midday sun. Pack lightweight neutral layers (tan, khaki, olive). Avoid dark navy blue or pitch black garments which attract woodland tsetse flies.

Frequently Asked Questions About This Expedition

Can this itinerary and its daily pace be customized?

Yes. Every Rihana expedition is 100% private and tailor-made. You have exclusive private use of your custom 4x4 Land Cruiser and master naturalist guide. Whether you wish to spend an entire morning tracking a single cheetah coalition, linger at a river crossing, or return early to enjoy your lodge plunge pool, your daily schedule is entirely flexible.

What standard of accommodation is included?

We partner exclusively with five-star wilderness lodges and luxury tented camps (such as Four Seasons Safari Lodge, Singita, Lemala, Elewana Collection, and Sanctuary Retreats). These properties feature plush king beds, ensuite private bathrooms with hot running water and flush toilets, fine dining, and panoramic savannah views.

Can we add a sunrise hot air balloon flight?

Yes! A dawn hot air balloon safari over the Serengeti or Maasai Mara can be seamlessly integrated into this itinerary. You will float silently above wildlife herds as the sun rises over the savannah, followed by a celebratory champagne bush breakfast in the wild.

What emergency medical protections are provided in remote areas?

Every guest traveling with Rihana Signature Safari is automatically enrolled in AMREF Flying Doctors 24/7 air ambulance evacuation coverage. In the event of a medical emergency, a dedicated emergency aircraft with medical personnel will dispatch to the nearest airstrip and airlift you directly to Nairobi's premier trauma centers.
