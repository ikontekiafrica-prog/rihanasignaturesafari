Laikipia Plateau • Mount Kenya Foothills

An Unrivaled Lewa Wildlife Conservancy & Laikipia Safari Experience

Sprawled across the high-altitude Laikipia Plateau in the shadow of Mount Kenya, Lewa Wildlife Conservancy and neighboring Ol Pejeta represent Africa's foremost conservation success stories. Together, they safeguard the highest concentration of critically endangered black and white rhinos in East Africa, including the last two northern white rhinos on Earth.

The Rhino Sanctuary of East Africa

Lewa safeguards 14% of Kenya's entire black rhino population. Guests encounter wild black and white rhinos grazing peacefully on open rolling hills with Mount Kenya as a dramatic backdrop.

Official Conservation Management: Inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in recognition of pioneer conservation management, community healthcare, and zero-poaching security operations.

Seasonal Guide: When to Plan Your Expedition

  • Dry Season Wildlife Gathering: June to October & December to March: Superb game tracking weather across the open Laikipia grasslands.
  • Calving & Green Season: April to May & November: Greening of the plateau with dramatic cloud formations around Mount Kenya.
  • Traveler Tip: The high altitude provides crisp, bug-free evenings perfect for open fire pits and stargazing.

Signature Activities in This Sanctuary

Behind-the-Scenes Rhino Tracking

Ride in private 4x4s alongside tracker scouts monitoring endangered black and white rhinos.

Horseback Safaris Alongside Zebras

Experience horseback riding across open plains, approaching wildlife that show no fear of horses.

Vintage Open-Cockpit Biplane Flights

Fly in an authentic open-cockpit biplane for a breathtaking low-altitude flight across rivers and gorges.

Sweetwaters Chimpanzee Sanctuary Visit

Meet rescued orphaned chimpanzees rehabilitated in lush natural enclosures at neighboring Ol Pejeta.

Transit Logistics: How to Reach This Sanctuary

A 45-minute flight from Nairobi Wilson Airport (WIL) directly into Lewa Downs Airstrip.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can we see both black and white rhinos in Lewa?
Yes! Lewa is one of the few places in Africa where both black and white rhinos roam together in high numbers.
Is horse riding suitable for beginners?
Yes, expert equestrian guides match horses to each guest's skill level, from gentle walking rides to advanced canters.
What luxury properties are available in Lewa?
We curate stays at Sirikoi Lodge, Lewa Wilderness, and Elewana Lewa Safari Camp.

Sanctuary Fast Facts

  • Location & Area 250 sq km (62,000 acres) UNESCO World Heritage Site
  • Best Time to Visit Year-Round. The high elevation provides a pleasant, temperate climate throughout the year.
  • Climate & Pacing Temperate high-plateau climate (1,700m); sunny days (24°C) and refreshing nights.
  • Key Wildlife The Last Two Northern White Rhinos (Najin & Fatu), Over 200 Black & White Rhinos, Jackson's Hartebeests & Grevy's Zebras
  • Governing Authority Lewa Wildlife Conservancy & UNESCO World Heritage
