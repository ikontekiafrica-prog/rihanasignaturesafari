An Unrivaled Zanzibar Archipelago & Private Islands Safari Experience

Floating in the warm, azure waters of the Indian Ocean, the Zanzibar Archipelago—encompassing Unguja, Pemba, Chumbe, and exclusive private Mnemba Island—is the ultimate sensory conclusion to a rugged bush safari. Here, centuries-old Swahili and Arabic palace heritage in Stone Town merges with powder-white coral sands, offshore dolphin reefs, and private oceanfront villas.

Mnemba Atoll Coral Reefs & Private Island Villas

Surrounded by a protected marine sanctuary, Mnemba Atoll offers world-class scuba diving and snorkeling with green sea turtles, bottlenose dolphins, and hundreds of tropical reef species, paired with total barefoot luxury.

Official Conservation Management: Marine habitats around Mnemba, Chumbe, and Menai Bay are protected marine conservation reserves. Rihana Signature Safari arranges certified ethical dolphin viewing and reef conservation excursions.

Seasonal Guide: When to Plan Your Expedition

  • Dry Season Wildlife Gathering: June to October: Crystal-clear diving visibility, calm seas, and balmy breezes make this the quintessential beach holiday season.
  • Calving & Green Season: December to March: Warmest tropical sunshine and glassy ocean conditions, ideal for dhow sailing and deep-sea game fishing.
  • Traveler Tip: April to May experiences the long rains; we recommend private oceanfront pool villas for secluded relaxation.

Direct 45-minute scheduled or private charter flights from Serengeti bush airstrips, Arusha, or Dar es Salaam land directly at Abeid Amani Karume International Airport (ZNZ).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Zanzibar easily combined with a Serengeti safari?
Yes, this is our most popular signature pairing. Guests can fly directly from a Serengeti bush airstrip to Zanzibar in under 2 hours with zero road hassle.
Which coast has the best beaches in Zanzibar?
The northeast coast (Matemwe, Nungwi, Kendwa) offers deep turquoise water that is minimally affected by low tides, alongside ultra-private cliffside villas.
What luxury properties does Rihana partner with in Zanzibar?
We curate stays at Zuri Zanzibar, Zanzibar White Sand Luxury Villas (Relais & Châteaux), The Palms, and exclusive private island buyouts at Mnemba Island.
