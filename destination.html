Private Sky Logistics

Seamless Flight Charters Between Sanctuaries

At Rihanna Signature Safari, we do not subject our guests to grueling 8-hour highway transfers between remote parks. We connect East Africa's greatest sanctuaries via private Cessna Grand Caravan and King Air bush charters, landing directly on secluded wilderness airstrips (Seronera, Grumeti, Kogatende, Amboseli, Mara North, and Zanzibar).

Upon touchdown, your private custom-built 4x4 Land Cruiser awaits on the tarmac, complete with refrigerated refreshments, onboard Wi-Fi, 360-degree photographic roof hatches, and your dedicated master naturalist guide.
