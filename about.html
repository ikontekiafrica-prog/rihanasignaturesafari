Crafting East Africa’s Most Extraordinary Journeys

Founded on a lifelong passion for East Africa's breathtaking landscapes, Rihana Signature Safari curates unforgettable travel experiences across Tanzania and Kenya.

For Tanzania, our offerings cover the complete journey: prime wildlife safaris across the Serengeti and Ngorongoro, high-altitude mountain trekking up Mount Kilimanjaro, and serene tropical island relaxation on Zanzibar. For Kenya, we focus dedicatedly on authentic, world-renowned wildlife safaris across the Maasai Mara, Amboseli, and Tsavo reserves.

Visionary Leadership

Passionate Guardians of East Africa's Wild Heritage

Founded by Tanzanian tourism entrepreneur and Environmental Engineer Redempotius Respicius Nalisis, Rihana Signature Safari was born out of an uncompromising commitment to showcasing Africa's pristine wilderness through the highest standards of luxury, conservation responsibility, and bespoke personalization.

Headquartered in Dar es Salaam with operational reserve pavilions spanning Arusha, Moshi, and Zanzibar, Redempotius brings years of deep expertise across Tanzania's safari terrain. His background in environmental engineering underpins Rihana's strict sustainable tourism ethos—directing lodge partnerships toward low-impact solar power, supporting armed anti-poaching ranger units, and ensuring that each journey leaves a lasting, positive footprint on local communities.
